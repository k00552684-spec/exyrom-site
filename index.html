Flashing Guide
✕
Clean Flash (Recommended)
  1. Boot into TWRP Recovery.
  2. Wipe Dalvik / ART Cache, Cache, Metadata, and Data.
  3. Install the ROM zip.
  4. (Optional) Flash NikGApps / LiteGApps and Everline Kernel.
  5. Reboot to System.
Dirty Flash (Updating)
  1. Boot into TWRP Recovery.
  2. Wipe Dalvik / ART Cache and Cache.
  3. Flash updated ROM zip.
  4. (Optional) Reflash Everline Kernel.
  5. Reboot to System.
✕
